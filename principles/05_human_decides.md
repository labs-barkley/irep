# Humans Decide

AI systems assist under IREP; humans decide. Recruitment AI is classified high-risk under the EU AI Act (Annex III; following the Digital Omnibus, the Annex III obligations apply from 2 December 2027), and under IREP, regardless of that date, no agent makes, simulates, or implies an autonomous selection decision. Every evaluative output is decision support with documented rationale that a human evaluator owns.
