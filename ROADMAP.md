# Roadmap

## The registry (issues #4, #9, #15)

**What the registry is.** The cross-organisation record of pre-registered rubrics with deferred outcome reporting. It is the institutional layer: it makes conformance verifiable by a third party, and it is the only place where the retention claim can be powered (Baelde 2026, Appendix A; issue #4). The badge is proof of enrolment, nothing more.

**Entry schema (v0.2 proposal).** Rubric (items, anchors, essential items and their minimum levels, weights), aggregation rule and threshold (spec §8.2, §8.3), pre-registration timestamp anchored by an external time-stamping authority (RFC 3161), allocation rule and exploration parameters (spec §8.4), inter-rater agreement, share of not-assessable weight per candidate, applicant-pool score distribution, and deferred outcome fields: separation at 12 months split voluntary and involuntary, the 6-month binary manager judgement, and objective output where it exists (issue #12). Ratings enter as secondary, with rater fixed effects.

**Trust tiers and what each permits an organisation to say.** Self-declared: "we use IREP"; nothing about outcomes. Peer-reviewed: a second implementer has checked the pre-registration record; "our rubrics are pre-registered". Registry-audited: the entry is in the registry with its external timestamp and the outcome fields are reported; "our conformance is third-party verifiable". No tier permits "IREP improved our hiring" until the registry publishes pooled results with their confidence intervals.

**Blocking preconditions (issue #15).** (1) The formal re-identification analysis of trajectory fields (issue #2), including l-diversity on outcome fields, since an employer must not be able to recognise its own rejected candidate and read that candidate's outcome elsewhere (Sweeney 2002; Machanavajjhala et al. 2007; Johary et al. 2026). (2) A controllership analysis of the actual roles of the registry operator and of the participating employers, under the EDPB Guidelines 07/2020 on the concepts of controller and processor (EDPB 2021), completed before the first record lands. Joint controllership is expected for employers who pool records for the registry's common purposes, since choosing a shared system for converging purposes can amount to a joint decision on means; it is established by that analysis, not declared. The operator's own role follows from the same analysis, and the Article 26 arrangement, where required, is written accordingly. (3) Differentially private release of any pooled aggregate (Gould 2024). Nothing about the registry ships before the three are specified.

**The regulatory clock.** Annex III obligations for recruitment systems apply from 2 December 2027 under Regulation (EU) 2026/1744. The registry is designed to be a documentation and logging layer of the kind those obligations call for. It is never a compliance product, and no implementation is compliant by virtue of using IREP (issue #9).
